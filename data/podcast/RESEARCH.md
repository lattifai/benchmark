## 5 Selected Podcasts

### 1. Lex Fridman Podcast
- **Channel**: [@lexfridman](https://www.youtube.com/@lexfridman)
- **Typical duration**: 2–5 hours
- **Style**: Long-form deep-dive interviews on science, AI, philosophy
- **Notable AI guests**: Sam Altman, Dario Amodei, Demis Hassabis, Sundar Pichai

### 2. Dwarkesh Podcast
- **Channel**: [@DwarkeshPatel](https://www.youtube.com/@DwarkeshPatel)
- **Typical duration**: 1–3 hours
- **Style**: Intellectual deep-dives with AI researchers and tech leaders
- **Notable AI guests**: Ilya Sutskever, Andrej Karpathy, Dario Amodei, Elon Musk, Satya Nadella

### 3. No Priors (Elad Gil & Sarah Guo)
- **Channel**: YouTube (search "No Priors podcast")
- **Typical duration**: 20–60 minutes
- **Style**: Concise AI industry interviews with founders and executives
- **Notable AI guests**: Jensen Huang, ElevenLabs CEO, Rivian CEO

### 4. Machine Learning Street Talk (MLST)
- **Channel**: [@MachineLearningStreetTalk](https://www.youtube.com/@MachineLearningStreetTalk)
- **Typical duration**: 40 min – 3+ hours
- **Style**: Technical AI discussions with researchers
- **Notable AI guests**: Jeremy Howard, Karl Friston, Llion Jones (Transformer co-inventor)

### 5. Latent Space (swyx & Alessio)
- **Channel**: YouTube (search "Latent Space podcast")
- **Typical duration**: 30–80 minutes
- **Style**: By and for AI engineers, covering tools, models, and infrastructure
- **Notable AI guests**: Greg Brockman, Jeff Dean, Aaron Levie
